TRANSFORMERS

More than meets the eye, Autobots rise to protect worlds, transforming hope into unstoppable legend.

THE TRANSFORMERS

Forged from living metal, the Transformers are powerful alien robots from Cybertron who can transform into vehicles, weapons, and machines. Divided into Autobots and Decepticons, they battle for freedom, power, and the fate of worlds across the universe.
